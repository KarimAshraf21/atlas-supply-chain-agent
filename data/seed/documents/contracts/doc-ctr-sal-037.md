# Framework Supply & Delivery Agreement — Lone Star Storage Partners

**Contract ID:** CTR-SAL-037
**Buyer:** Lone Star Storage Partners
**Seller:** Nilewave Energy
**Term:** 2025-01-20 to 2028-01-20
**Estimated value:** USD 115,982,000

## 1. Scope
The Seller shall supply the following: Pharos 2.5 MWh Grid Battery Container; Pharos 5 MWh Grid Battery Container.

## 2. Delivery
Deliveries are made DAP at the Buyer's nominated site. Lead times are confirmed per purchase order; the Seller
shall notify the Buyer of any expected delay of more than three (3) days.

## 3. Liquidated damages
For each day of late delivery the Seller shall pay liquidated damages of 0.3% of the value of
the delayed delivery, capped at 10.0% of that delivery's value.

## 5. Force majeure
The parties expressly agree that this Agreement contains no force-majeure relief for delays arising from logistics or shipping-route disruptions. The Seller bears all transport risk until delivery and remains liable for liquidated damages irrespective of the cause of delay.

## 6. Minimum volumes
No minimum volume commitment applies.

## 7. Governing law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales. Disputes are settled by arbitration in London (LCIA).
