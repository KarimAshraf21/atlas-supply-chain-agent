# Master Supply Agreement — Silesia E-Mobility Cables

**Contract ID:** CTR-SUP-023
**Buyer:** Nilewave Energy
**Seller:** Silesia E-Mobility Cables
**Term:** 2025-08-16 to 2027-08-16
**Estimated value:** USD 43,301,000

## 1. Scope
The Seller shall supply the following: CCS2 charging cable & connector.

## 2. Delivery
Deliveries are made DAP at the Buyer's nominated site. Lead times are confirmed per purchase order; the Seller
shall notify the Buyer of any expected delay of more than three (3) days.

## 3. Liquidated damages
For each day of late delivery the Seller shall pay liquidated damages of 0.1% of the value of
the delayed delivery, capped at 5.0% of that delivery's value.

## 4. Exclusivity
Silesia E-Mobility Cables is the exclusive source for the Products during the Term. The Buyer may only qualify alternative sources after a documented supply failure exceeding thirty (30) days.

## 5. Force majeure
Neither party shall be liable for delay or failure to perform caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including war, armed conflict, acts of terrorism, blockade, closure of international shipping routes, epidemic, natural disaster or governmental action, provided that the affected party notifies the other within five (5) business days and uses commercially reasonable efforts to mitigate.

## 6. Minimum volumes
The Buyer commits to a minimum of 11,000 units per year.

## 7. Governing law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales. Disputes are settled by arbitration in London (LCIA).
