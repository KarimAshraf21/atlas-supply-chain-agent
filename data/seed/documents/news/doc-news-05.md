New end-user licensing requirements have slowed graphite anode exports by an estimated 40%.

Licences now take 45 working days and require end-use declarations. Cell makers outside China report inventories of anode material falling below six weeks.

Affected: Spherical graphite anode, Heilong Graphite.

Supply-chain analysts advise manufacturers with exposure to review safety stock, qualify alternative sources and check force-majeure provisions in customer contracts.
