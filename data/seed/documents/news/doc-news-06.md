Restrictions on gallium exports tighten supply for microcontroller and power-device makers.

Spot gallium prices outside China rose 55% in a month; chipmakers are drawing down safety stocks.

Affected: High-purity gallium, Yunnan Gallium Refinery.

Supply-chain analysts advise manufacturers with exposure to review safety stock, qualify alternative sources and check force-majeure provisions in customer contracts.
