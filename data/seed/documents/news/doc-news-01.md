Major carriers suspended Red Sea transits after a series of drone and missile strikes near Bab el-Mandeb. Asia–Europe and Asia–Red Sea services are diverting around the Cape of Good Hope.

War-risk insurance premiums have risen to 0.7% of hull value and spot rates on Asia–Mediterranean lanes have jumped 140% in three weeks. Carriers say diversions will last at least until mid-January.

Affected: Bab el-Mandeb Strait.

Supply-chain analysts advise manufacturers with exposure to review safety stock, qualify alternative sources and check force-majeure provisions in customer contracts.
