Industrial zones face rolling two-hour power cuts while gas supplies to power plants are constrained.

Factories with on-site generation are exempt from the second daily cut.

Affected: Sokhna Gigafactory, 10th of Ramadan Power Electronics Plant.

Supply-chain analysts advise manufacturers with exposure to review safety stock, qualify alternative sources and check force-majeure provisions in customer contracts.
