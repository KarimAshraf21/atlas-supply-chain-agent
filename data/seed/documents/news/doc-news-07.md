A strike ballot covering lashing crews could halt container handling at Europe's largest port.

Unions are demanding inflation-linked pay and automation guarantees; talks resume on 1 December.

Affected: Rotterdam.

Supply-chain analysts advise manufacturers with exposure to review safety stock, qualify alternative sources and check force-majeure provisions in customer contracts.
