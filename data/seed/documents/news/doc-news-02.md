Reduced northbound and southbound convoys add waiting time at both canal entrances.

Average waiting time at the southern anchorage has risen from 11 to 70 hours; the canal authority has offered rebates to carriers that keep their Suez rotations.

Affected: Suez Canal.

Supply-chain analysts advise manufacturers with exposure to review safety stock, qualify alternative sources and check force-majeure provisions in customer contracts.
