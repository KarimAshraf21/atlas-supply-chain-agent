Meteorologists expect a category-4 typhoon to hit southern Taiwan; port and fab shutdowns are likely.

Kaohsiung port has issued a level-2 alert. Semiconductor plants in the southern science park typically halt production for 2–4 days during typhoon landfall.

Affected: Kaohsiung, Taiwan Strait, FormoSiC Semiconductor.

Supply-chain analysts advise manufacturers with exposure to review safety stock, qualify alternative sources and check force-majeure provisions in customer contracts.
