Analysts put a one-in-five chance on a multi-week disruption to Gulf container traffic.

A closure would cut off Jebel Ali and Dammam from sea freight; overland routes from Red Sea ports add 4–6 days.

Affected: Strait of Hormuz.

Supply-chain analysts advise manufacturers with exposure to review safety stock, qualify alternative sources and check force-majeure provisions in customer contracts.
