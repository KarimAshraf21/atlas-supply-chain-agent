Lenders to the copper-foil maker are weighing a debt-for-equity swap after covenant breaches.

The company supplies 6 µm copper foil to several Asian LFP cell makers and has no second plant.

Affected: Daegu Copper Foil.

Supply-chain analysts advise manufacturers with exposure to review safety stock, qualify alternative sources and check force-majeure provisions in customer contracts.
